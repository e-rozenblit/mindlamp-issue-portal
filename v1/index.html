For the developer meeting

Issues ranked by what study teams report. The priority signal is a starting point for discussion, not a decision. Every part of it is shown so you can disagree with it.

Until teams start reporting, the impact ratings on starter issues are provisional: set by the platform team from existing evidence (troubleshooting records, past reports, a partner study's log).

Signal = severity (what it stops, 0 to 3, plus what it does to data, 0 to 3) + breadth (1 team = 1, 2 to 3 = 2, 4 to 6 = 3, 7 or more = 4) + boosts (up to 3) + reports this month (up to 3).

Proposals

What this is

Many problems with mindLAMP are solved or worked around inside each study and never reach the platform team. This portal collects them in one shared format, so the team can see how common a problem is, what it costs studies, and which fixes matter most. It also lets teams see what others are running into and which workarounds help.

What gets shared

Records about individual participants stay with your team, under your own IRB. The portal only receives summaries: how many participants, what kind of phone and setup, what it affected, what fixed it.

A contributor community

Everyone here contributes: a coordinator who documents a problem or a workaround, an analyst who spots bad data, a developer who fixes it. Contributions are credited by name on the issue, and the platform team marks issues and workarounds it has confirmed.

Questions for you: How much recognition would make this worth your team's time (credit on issues, acknowledgment in release notes, a citable contributor list)? Should contributors themselves be verified, or only the issues and workarounds? Is "contributor" the right word for what coordinators do here? Answer in the feedback form below.
